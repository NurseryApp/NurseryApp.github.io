Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nursery handles information when you use the Nursery application.

Effective: August 31, 2026

1. About Nursery

Nursery is a baby-care organization application intended for parents, guardians and caregivers. Nursery allows users to record and organize information such as feeding, sleep, diaper changes, growth and other care information.

2. Information Nursery May Process

The information processed by Nursery depends on the features you choose to use and may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
  • Baby profile information, including name, date of birth, feeding preferences and profile information.
  • Care records you enter, including feeding, sleep, diaper, growth, food, medicine and notes.
  • Information you voluntarily enter in baby-information areas, which may include allergy, pediatrician, insurance, emergency-contact or guardian information.
  • Shared Nursery information required to synchronize records between approved household members.
  • Caregiver-access information required to provide temporary caregiver functionality.
  • Purchase and subscription entitlement information supplied by Apple for Nursery Plus.
  • Technical information processed by service providers as necessary for authentication, synchronization, security and operation of Nursery.

3. How Information Is Used

Nursery uses information to operate and provide features you choose to use, including:

  • Recording and displaying care history
  • Synchronizing Shared Nursery information
  • Providing authorized caregiver access
  • Providing reminders and organizational tools
  • Generating personalized patterns and predictions
  • Providing Ask Nursery functionality
  • Maintaining account security
  • Determining Nursery Plus entitlement status

4. Advertising and Sale of Information

Nursery does not sell your personal information.

Nursery does not use baby-care information for cross-app advertising or behavioral advertising.

5. Ask Nursery and Predictions

Nursery may use care records already stored in the application to answer questions, summarize recent history and provide predictions or pattern information.

These functions are intended as organizational and informational tools. They are not medical advice and should not be used to diagnose a condition, determine whether medication is appropriate or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Cloud Synchronization

When cloud synchronization or Shared Nursery features are used, Nursery may store selected account and nursery data using Google Firebase services so authorized users can access synchronized nursery information across supported devices.

Nursery uses authentication and access controls designed to restrict synchronized nursery information to authorized users and permitted caregivers.

7. Apple Services and Purchases

Nursery Plus purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and in-app purchase systems.

Nursery may receive information from Apple indicating whether a purchase or subscription entitlement is active. Nursery does not receive your complete payment-card details from Apple.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Nursery provides an in-app process for requesting deletion of your account and associated data.

In Nursery, this can be accessed through:

Nursery Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data

If you are the only member of a Shared Nursery, synchronized nursery records associated with that nursery may be deleted as part of the account-deletion process.

If other authorized household members remain in a Shared Nursery, shared baby-care history may remain available to those members where necessary to preserve their legitimate shared records. References associated with the deleted account may be removed or anonymized where practical.

Deleting a Nursery account does not automatically cancel an active Nursery Plus subscription managed by Apple.

9. Security

Nursery uses technical safeguards including authenticated account access and access-control rules intended to restrict synchronized nursery information to authorized users.

However, no internet-connected system, service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Caregiver and Shared Access

If you choose to share a Nursery, information may become accessible to other household members you authorize.

If you provide temporary caregiver access, selected nursery information and functions may be made available to that caregiver for the access period you authorize.

You are responsible for sharing invitations and access credentials only with people you trust.

11. Children's Privacy

Nursery is designed for use by parents, guardians and caregivers. Nursery accounts are not intended to be created or operated independently by children.

Information about a child is entered and managed by the parent, guardian or caregiver using Nursery.

12. Third-Party Service Providers

Nursery may rely on third-party service providers to operate specific functionality.

These may include:

  • Apple — App Store distribution, in-app purchases and subscription management.
  • Google Firebase — authentication and synchronized cloud data services.

These services process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the features you use, you may:

  • Stop using cloud-sharing functionality
  • Revoke caregiver access
  • Edit or delete certain care records
  • Delete baby profiles where available
  • Delete your Nursery account and data
  • Manage Nursery Plus through Apple

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nursery may update this Privacy Policy as features, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
