Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore Nursery Plus?

Open Nursery and go to the Nursery Plus or subscription screen. Use the Restore Purchases option while signed into the Apple ID that originally purchased the subscription.

How do I cancel Nursery Plus?

Nursery Plus subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel an active subscription through your Apple ID subscription settings on your device.

Does deleting Nursery cancel my subscription?

No. Deleting your Nursery account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed through Apple. Subscriptions must be managed separately through Apple.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Nursery, open:

Nursery Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data

Follow the instructions shown in the app.

What is Shared Nursery?

Shared Nursery allows approved household members to access synchronized nursery information. Depending on your plan and app version, Nursery may also provide temporary caregiver access.

I joined a Shared Nursery but don't see the data.

Confirm that you are signed into the correct account, verify your internet connection and reopen Nursery. If the issue continues, contact support and include a description of what happened.

Does Nursery provide medical advice?

No. Nursery is designed to organize information and provide general informational tools. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

For urgent medical concerns: Contact your child's pediatrician or appropriate emergency medical services. Do not rely on Nursery for an emergency or time-sensitive medical decision.
